📖 How To Play

  1. Create a room and share the room code.
  2. 3 to 8 players join the room.
  3. One player secretly becomes the Hacker.
  4. Everyone else becomes Analysts.
  5. Analysts know the topic.
  6. The Hacker does NOT know the topic.
  7. Everyone submits one clue.
  8. Do NOT reveal the topic directly.
  9. Vote for who you think is the Hacker.
  10. If the Hacker is identified, Analysts win.
  11. If the Hacker avoids detection, Hacker wins.
